# Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship

## About the Internship

This repository contains the projects completed as part of the **Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship**. These projects demonstrate practical knowledge of Python, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, Power BI, Power Query, and DAX.

The objective of these projects is to analyze retail sales data, identify meaningful business insights, and present key performance indicators through an interactive dashboard.

---

## Project 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Superstore Retail Sales

**Tools & Technologies:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

### Project Overview
Performed Exploratory Data Analysis on a Superstore Retail Sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, regional performance, and discount impact.

### Activities Performed
- Inspected and understood the dataset structure.
- Handled missing values and duplicate records.
- Performed data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Analyzed overall sales, profit, and quantity.
- Studied monthly sales trends and seasonality.
- Analyzed sales and profit by category, sub-category, and region.
- Identified top customers based on sales.
- Examined the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Created visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn.
- Identified important business findings and recommendations.

### Key Business Insights
- Technology was the highest-selling category.
- The West region generated the highest profit.
- November 2018 recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Sean Miller was the top customer by sales.
- Higher discount levels were associated with negative profitability in several discount groups.

### Deliverables
- `TASK1.ipynb` – Jupyter Notebook containing Python code, analysis, and visualizations.
- `superstore_cleaned.csv` – Cleaned Superstore dataset.

## Project 2: Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard – Power BI

**Tools & Technologies:** Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX

### Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Superstore sales and profit performance. The dashboard presents key business metrics and helps users explore sales trends, product categories, customer segments, and regional performance.

### Activities Performed
- Imported the Superstore dataset into Power BI.
- Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query.
- Checked data types, missing values, errors, and duplicate records.
- Created DAX measures for key performance indicators.
- Developed KPI cards to display business metrics.
- Created monthly sales trend visualizations.
- Analyzed sales by category and customer segment.
- Evaluated profit performance across regions.
- Identified top-performing products.
- Added interactive date and region slicers.

### Dashboard Features
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin
- Average Discount
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Interactive Date and Region Filters

### Deliverables
- `Task2.pbix` – Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- `Datheon_Sales_Profit_Dashboard.pdf` – PDF export of the dashboard.
